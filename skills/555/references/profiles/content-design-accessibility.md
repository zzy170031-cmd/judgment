# P07 Content / Design / Accessibility

Use for writing, images, video, interfaces, presentations, brand, character
continuity, or user-experience acceptance.

For interface or design review, read [the design audit protocol](../design-audit.md).
It combines task-based critique, measurable browser checks, before/after
preservation, and explicit limits. The optional web observer is evidence for
this profile, not a replacement for independent review or a complete
accessibility certification.

## Criteria And Required Evidence

- Inspect original assets and the real target size, resolution, playback, or
  interaction context rather than thumbnails or prose descriptions.
- Check audience, information hierarchy, task completion, responsive states,
  keyboard/screen-reader access, color, and other applicable accessibility needs.
- Check identity, terminology, brand, timeline, character, shot, and
  cross-page/cross-shot continuity.
- Verify source, permission, likeness, copyright, trademark, cultural context,
  and platform constraints.
- Declare full versus sampled review, every asset actually inspected, and all
  untouched material.
- Separate subjective preference from reproducible defect and name the target
  audience and acceptance method for recommendations.

## Role Seeds

Creative/design owner, target audience, accessibility reviewer, continuity
reviewer, rights/source verifier.

## Boundary

A sampled or thumbnail review cannot support a full-resolution, full-corpus, or
interaction-complete acceptance claim.
