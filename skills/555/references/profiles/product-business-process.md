# P06 Product / Business / Process

Use for product proposals, business processes, team workflows, procurement,
cost/benefit decisions, or operating mechanisms.

## Criteria And Required Evidence

- Define the user's real job, current baseline, failure cost, and observable
  success outcome.
- Map main flow, exceptions, manual fallback, cross-team handoffs, decision
  rights, and accountable owner.
- Account for time, budget, maintenance, training, migration, adoption friction,
  opportunity cost, and downstream cost transfer.
- Require a bounded pilot, stop condition, rollback, scale-up condition, and
  post-launch feedback loop where uncertainty is material.
- Every recommendation needs an owner, exact action, dependency, acceptance
  evidence, and a consequence if the evidence fails.

## Role Seeds

Target user, process owner, delivery owner, cost/finance viewpoint, downstream
operator.

## Boundary

Do not confuse a plausible design with proven adoption, realized savings, or
organizational approval.
