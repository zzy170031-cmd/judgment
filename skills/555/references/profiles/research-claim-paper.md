# P02 Research / Claim / Paper

Use for papers, reports, public-web research, policy material, factual claims,
or decisions justified by literature.

## Criteria And Required Evidence

- Decompose the conclusion into testable Claims; a source's existence does not
  prove that it supports the Claim.
- Prefer original papers, official documents, standards, datasets, and
  first-party records; label secondary summaries and Judgment inferences.
- Trace citation chains and common upstream sources. Several pages repeating one
  paper, press release, or dataset form one origin group.
- Check version, publication date, event date, definitions, methodology,
  limitations, applicability, and credible counter-studies.
- Record search cutoff, platforms searched, inaccessible full text, and
  unsearched or excluded scope.

## Role Seeds

Domain researcher, methodologist, source verifier, counter-evidence researcher,
deployment practitioner.

## Boundary

Report evidence-backed risk and uncertainty; do not turn source volume into a
legal, scientific, or policy certainty it cannot support.
