# P09 Regulated / Legal / Financial / Medical Decision

Use when an incorrect recommendation could create material legal, contractual,
financial, medical, regulatory, or personal harm.

## Criteria And Required Evidence

- Freeze jurisdiction, affected party, governing authority, version, effective
  date, definitions, exceptions, and evidence cutoff.
- Separate verified facts, evidence-backed risk interpretation, value choice,
  and the final licensed or organizational conclusion.
- Check record retention, explanation duty, conflicts of interest, privacy, and
  appeal or second-review paths for affected people.
- Treat uncertainty as decision material when it changes action; identify the
  licensed professional or authorized organizational owner who must sign.
- Prefer official and primary sources, and state when only secondary summaries
  or inaccessible originals are available.

## Role Seeds

Applicable domain expert, compliance/records owner, affected-person advocate,
independent evidence verifier, final licensed or organizational decision owner.

## Boundary

Judgment prepares evidence, risks, questions, and an executable verification
plan. It does not impersonate legal, medical, financial, statutory-audit, or
other formally licensed advice.
